Workflow
  1. Build — choose the client folder, Standalone or Claude, build all sequences. Duplicates are checked by content first.
  2. Grade — exposure preset, live Lumetri, Remove green. Copy one clip's Overall, select other clips, Paste. Everything is live; skin and green Color Shift stay in Premiere's Color tab.
  3. Review — fix flags, save grade references, then Final check & save learning.
  4. Export — UAI Smart Export.
